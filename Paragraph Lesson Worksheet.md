## Activity 1: Identifying Components of a Paragraph

**Instructions:** Read the paragraph and identify the following:
1. Topic Sentence
2. Major Supporting Details
3. Minor Supporting Details

> Time management is a critical skill for university success. It allows students to balance their academic responsibilities with their personal life. For example, setting up a weekly schedule helps prioritize tasks like studying and assignments. Additionally, managing time effectively reduces stress because it prevents last-minute cramming. Taking breaks between tasks also helps maintian focus and avoid burnout.

**Instructions:** Write a topic sentence for each idea below, then add major and minor supporting details.

1. **Idea:** The benefits of being multilingual.
    * **Topic Sentence:** Being multilingual can be beneficial because it can increase career and professional opportunities.
    * **Major Detail 1:** Multilingual employees have expanded job opportunities.
    * **Minor Detail:** Many jobs look for employees who can speak multiple languages, especially in global markets.
    * **Minor Detail:** Jobs such as translation, and tourism require employees to be multilingual.
    * **Major Detail 2:** Additionally, multilingual employees often get higher wages.
    * **Minor Detail:** They stand out from other employees because they have the ability to be fluent in multiple languages.
    * **Minor Detail:** For example, international business, customer service, and tech industries has high demands for multilingual employees.

3. **Idea:** The challenges of working while studying.
    * **Topic Sentence:** Working while studying could be challenging as it demands the student to be good at time management.
    * **Major Detail 1:** Students might not be getting enough rest while they are prioritizing work and study at the same time.
    * **Minor Detail:** This could cause a student to be exhausted or not able to concentrate when they are working or studying, lowering their efficiency.
    * **Major Detail 2:** Furthermore, the student is highly likely required to multitask throughout his academic journey.
    * **Minor Detail:** For example, students could be forced to think about their jobs while teachers are teaching in class.
